## What it does

The backend behind product traceability for enterprise and mid-market consumer-product brands. Products, batches and serialized units go through it, along with the authentication checks consumers run.

## What I built

- Multi-org, multi-user authentication.
- Dynamic provenance creation.
- DIY setup flows, so clients can onboard themselves.
- GS1 serialization and NFC-based product authentication APIs.
- BullMQ pipelines for scheduled reports, batch jobs, and email and SMS delivery, with retries.
- SAP integration APIs.

## Performance

Dashboard queries got 30% faster after I restructured the MongoDB aggregation pipelines: `$match` moved before `$lookup`, plus compound indexes on the high-volume queries.
