Production started failing in more than one place at once. Different services, different errors. That pattern usually means the problem isn't in any of them.

## Why one Redis can take down many services

In our platform, Redis sits underneath a lot of things:

- **BullMQ queues.** Scheduled reports, batch jobs, and email and SMS notifications all run as jobs in Redis.
- **Cache.** Hot reads are served from Redis instead of the database.

When Redis goes, everything that touches it fails too. The services look broken, but they're really waiting on the same dependency. That's what makes it a cascading failure.

## What I took from it

- **Many failures at once usually means one cause.** Look for the shared dependency.
- **Know what sits on Redis.** Queues and cache are easy to forget about until they stop.
- **Restore first, then write it down.** The write-up is where the lasting fix comes from.
